# Account reauthentication and automatic switching

The per-account warning action opens the existing official browser login flow for that account. Hover retains the full refresh error. A different account identity is rejected before registration is changed. Successful reauthentication updates the original registration, retaining its key, alias and usage history. Canonical desktop credentials are applied only through the existing switch action.

The toolbar's **Account auto-switch: Off / On** menu controls persisted automatic account switching, disabled by default (Korean: **계정 자동 전환: 꺼짐 / 켜짐**). Select a remaining-usage threshold of 5, 10 (default), 15 or 20 percent. The evaluator runs after the regular 60-second refresh. It requires one current account, successful observations no older than two minutes, matching bucket/window identities and positive durations, and a candidate with every reported window at least five percentage points above the threshold. Missing/zero/negative durations do not qualify even when both accounts omit the same value. A source at exactly the threshold does not trigger. An exhausted source can switch away; restricted candidates cannot be selected. Displaying a different quota in a card does not change this policy. Stale, failed, refreshing, missing or invalid quota data never initiates a handoff. A stopped/disconnected desktop, active login/switch/query, five-minute persisted cooldown or pending recovery also blocks automatic selection.

Choose **Best available automatically** (default) to maximize the lowest remaining percentage across the candidate's quota windows. This compares reported percentages, not inferred token counts or purchased credits. **Custom priority** chooses the first eligible account in the saved order. Each account's menu has **Priority 1/2/...**; choosing a position moves that account there, shifts the others and selects custom mode without enabling automation. The toolbar menu also shows the complete order. Invalid or restricted high-priority accounts are skipped; priority never bypasses freshness, minimum remaining, cooldown or recovery guards. Unranked/new accounts follow saved ranks in stable key order. Returning to automatic mode preserves the saved custom order.

Automatic handoff uses the same checkpointed backup, normal shutdown, account application, launch and eligible continuation coordinator as manual switching. Checkpoints are saved before shutdown and credential application. If failure or cancellation follows confirmed shutdown, the coordinator makes one bounded (35-second) attempt to reopen the recorded source/target desktop, without changing credentials or sending Continue. A failed/unconfirmed shutdown never invokes the launcher or applies credentials; its recovery record remains available. The checkpoint is retained even when reopening succeeds. A failed automatic handoff disables further automatic switching. **Recover** remains available on the recorded target even while the original account is still current, and other targets cannot replace the pending plan.

After the utility restarts, an interrupted automatic plan gets one persisted recovery attempt. Explicit user/lifetime cancellation prevents that automatic retry and retains manual Recover. Further failures require the visible Recover action; no indefinite timer/restart loop is allowed. Already completed chats and original Continue message identities survive recovery; ambiguous delivery retains the existing no-resend policy. Disabling automation affects future selection, not a checkpointed handoff already in progress. Recovery cannot guarantee launch when the client is missing or its IPC remains unavailable; the pending plan and error remain available for a later retry.

Both UIs label the subscription date **Expires** and calendar-day count **Days left**, with existing near-expiry colors. Missing values remain unavailable. Dates continue to come from subscription claims, not JWT expiry or an inferred billing date. Subscription information can be stale until the next successful login/refresh.

The shared presenter preserves official numbered Pro names rather than collapsing them into one family. [Official pricing](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/pricing) and [official plan names](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots#access), checked 2026-10-24, distinguish **Pro 100**, **Pro 200** and **Pro 500**. The official desktop26.1016.8622.0 `plan-names` module's `planUpgradeModal.subscription.numberedTiers` message maps `prolite` to **Pro 100**, `pro` to **Pro 200**, and `promax` to **Pro 500**. Existing explicit numeric/underscore aliases retain the matching tier. Legacy `team` displays as **Business**. This only formats the label: original account entitlements remain unchanged, and the number is the official tier name rather than a claim about the account's actual charge or billing currency. Unknown future identifiers remain visible.
