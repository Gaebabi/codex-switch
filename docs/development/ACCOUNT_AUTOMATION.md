# Account reauthentication and automatic switching

The per-account warning action opens the existing official browser login flow for that account. Hover retains the full refresh error. A different account identity is rejected before registration is changed. Successful reauthentication updates the original registration, retaining its key, alias and usage history. Canonical desktop credentials are applied only through the existing switch action.

The **Auto Switch** toolbar menu contains a persistent On/Off toggle and the next eligible account, with automation disabled by default. Select a remaining-usage threshold of 5, 10 (default), 15 or 20 percent. The evaluator runs after the regular 60-second refresh. It requires one current account, successful observations no older than two minutes, matching bucket/window identities and positive durations, and a candidate with every reported window at least five percentage points above the threshold. Missing/zero/negative durations do not qualify even when both accounts omit the same value. A source at exactly the threshold does not trigger. An exhausted source can switch away; restricted candidates cannot be selected. Displaying a different quota in a card does not change this policy. Stale, failed, refreshing, missing or invalid quota data never initiates a handoff. A stopped/disconnected desktop, active login/switch/query, five-minute persisted cooldown or pending recovery also blocks automatic selection.

Choose **Expires soonest first** (default) to consume the nearest future subscription expiry among eligible accounts, then maximize the lowest remaining percentage across quota windows. Missing or past expiry dates follow future dates; equal/unknown dates use remaining usage and stable account keys. This compares reported percentages, not inferred token counts or purchased credits. **Custom priority** chooses the first eligible account in the saved order. Each account's menu has **Priority 1/2/...**; choosing a position moves that account there, shifts the others and selects custom mode without enabling automation. The menu also shows the next eligible account even before the current quota crosses its threshold, using the same candidate guards/ranking. This read-only forecast does not reserve or start a handoff, can change after a refresh, and retains an existing pending-recovery target. It refreshes on menu opening and normal UI updates; there is no extra poller. No eligible account is explicit. The toolbar tooltip retains On/Off state and the forecast. The menu also shows the complete custom order. Invalid or restricted high-priority accounts are skipped; priority never bypasses freshness, minimum remaining, cooldown or recovery guards. Unranked/new accounts follow saved ranks in stable key order. Returning to automatic mode preserves the saved custom order.

Automatic handoff uses the same checkpointed backup, normal shutdown, account application, launch and eligible continuation coordinator as manual switching. Checkpoints are saved before shutdown and credential application. A transient I/O, discovery timeout or native launch failure gets at most **two additional retries**, with a one-second cancellable wait between attempts. The retry budget is shared across all stages and saved before each retry. Both UIs keep the target action disabled in its current switching/resuming state throughout these retries; **Recover** appears only after recovery is exhausted or a non-retryable refusal stops the operation. Confirmed names, applied credentials and completed chats are preserved. Each retry checks account identity again; a write that already reached the target is reconciled without applying it twice. Ambiguous Continue delivery can only be observed, never resent.

Cancellation and account mismatch stop the switch immediately. Integrity or permission refusals do not trigger a retry. After cancellation or a failed account application following confirmed shutdown, the coordinator retains one bounded (35-second) safety attempt to reopen the recorded source/target desktop without changing credentials or sending Continue. Exhausted launch retries do not add a fourth launch. A failed/unconfirmed shutdown never invokes the launcher or applies credentials; its recovery record remains available. A failed automatic handoff disables further automatic switching. **Recover** remains available on the recorded target even while the original account is still current, and other targets cannot replace the pending plan. Clicking Recover explicitly starts a fresh two-retry budget.

After the utility restarts, an interrupted automatic plan with no retry reserved gets one persisted recovery attempt, counted within the same two-retry budget. A plan that has already reserved a retry requires manual Recover after a utility restart. Explicit user/lifetime cancellation prevents automatic recovery and retains manual Recover. No indefinite timer/restart loop is allowed. Already completed chats and original Continue message identities survive recovery; ambiguous delivery retains the existing no-resend policy. Disabling automation affects future selection, not a checkpointed handoff already in progress. Recovery cannot guarantee launch when the client is missing or its IPC remains unavailable; the pending plan and error remain available for a later retry.

Both UIs label the subscription date **Expires** and calendar-day count **Days left**, with existing near-expiry colors. Missing values remain unavailable. Dates continue to come from subscription claims, not JWT expiry or an inferred billing date. Subscription information can be stale until the next successful login/refresh.

The shared presenter preserves official numbered Pro names rather than collapsing them into one family. [Official pricing](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/pricing) and [official plan names](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots#access), checked 2026-10-24, distinguish **Pro 100**, **Pro 200** and **Pro 500**. The official desktop26.1016.8622.0 `plan-names` module's `planUpgradeModal.subscription.numberedTiers` message maps `prolite` to **Pro 100**, `pro` to **Pro 200**, and `promax` to **Pro 500**. Existing explicit numeric/underscore aliases retain the matching tier. Legacy `team` displays as **Business**. This only formats the label: original account entitlements remain unchanged, and the number is the official tier name rather than a claim about the account's actual charge or billing currency. Unknown future identifiers remain visible.
